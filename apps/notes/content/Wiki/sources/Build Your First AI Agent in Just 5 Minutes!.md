# Build Your First AI Agent in Just 5 Minutes!

## Takeaways
- Thesis: agents are commoditizing; **marketing** is an early mass-adoption surface (LinkedIn content, social monitoring, scheduling).
- Agent = reasoning model + tools/APIs + memory/context + instructions/goals—not just a chat wrapper.
- Walkthrough builds a **LinkedIn Content Agent** via Hyperagent (Airtable): track X/Reddit, draft weekly commentary, automate ~45 min/day workflow.
- Low-code builders lower the bar but tie you to platform credits, integrations, and vendor opinionation.
- Heavily sponsored/tutorial tone; useful as pattern reference, not deep systems design.

## Notes
Illustrates no-code agent composition for solopreneur marketing—contrast with this vault's skill/MCP approach for knowledge work.
