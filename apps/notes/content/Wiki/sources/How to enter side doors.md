# How to enter side doors

## Takeaways

- The job market is a **search problem**, not a front-door application funnel — careers often start via side gates, warm intros, cold emails, and visible proof of work.
- A "job" is often a **need not yet written as a JD** — signal in the noise beats mass-applying to opaque titles on boards.
- **Side doors** include: public artifacts, mutual fascination, conversations, half-open windows, and one person willing to bet on another.
- **Legibility** matters — make your thinking, taste, and capabilities observable so the right people can find you.
- Cold outreach works when **specific and earnest**, not generic; demonstrate you understand their problem space.
- Students and early-career folks are over-taught the front door; building side-door muscle is a durable career skill.
