# Open in Safari [Shortcuts]

## Takeaways

- **Problem:** Arc (macOS default) + Arc Search (iOS) lack Safari-only shortcut actions and integrations.
- **Solution:** Apple Shortcuts workflow merges two Reddit-sourced shortcuts into one cross-platform action that opens the current page in Safari.
- **Usage:** iOS — Share Sheet → shortcut (pin to top); macOS — Share from Arc/Chrome → Shortcuts → select.
- **Workaround until Arc adds native "Open in Safari"** (like Firefox Focus).

## Notes

From tangiblelife.net's [Shortcuts] series. Useful when Safari-dependent automations (writing workflow shortcuts, Safari-specific actions) must run on a page viewed in Arc.
