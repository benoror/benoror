# OpenClaw, OpenAI and the future

## Takeaways

- **Peter Steinberger joins OpenAI** to work on bringing agents to everyone; mission shift: build an agent "even my mum can use."
- **OpenClaw stays open:** moving to a **foundation** structure — independent, open source, community-driven; OpenAI sponsors and commits to Steinberger dedicating time to it.
- **Not building a company:** Steinberger prefers building over the "creating-a-company game" (13 years prior); OpenAI = fastest path to scale + safety research + latest models.
- **Foundation goals:** thinkers, hackers, data ownership, multi-model/multi-vendor support.
- Viral "lobster" moment (ClawCon) catalyzed overwhelming inbound — investment pitches, advice, opportunities.

Feb 2026 post. Reconciles open-source independence with lab employment. "The claw is the law." Context for tracking OpenClaw governance alongside OpenAI agent roadmap.
