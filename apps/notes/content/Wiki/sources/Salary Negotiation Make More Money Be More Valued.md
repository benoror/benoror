# Salary Negotiation Make More Money Be More Valued

## Takeaways

- **Patrick McKenzie (kalzumeus):** engineers treat salary negotiation as virtue signaling; **5 minutes of negotiation** routinely moves compensation by thousands — compounding over career via raises, 401k, and future pegs.
- **Mindset shift:** rich people negotiate daily; middle-class Americans are socialized to view haggling as disreputable — but **~100% of gross income** is subject to salary negotiation vs. ~25% for housing.
- **Employer math is not yours:** fully-loaded cost ≈ **150–200% of salary**; $5k ask is mouse droppings to HR (~3% of loaded cost) but huge to you.
- **Recruiter (Bob) incentives:** Bob spends budget, not Bob's money; negotiating hard doesn't offend — may even help Bob's status/budget story.
- **Asymmetry:** companies exploit information advantage; job seekers default to employer's mental model at their own expense.
- **Claimed impact:** essay attributed to **$15M+** in negotiated raises (per clipping description).

~7,000-word classic (2012), aimed at engineers in hot US markets. Covers tactical advice beyond this summary (anchors, exploding offers, etc.) — read clipping for full playbook. Core thesis: negotiation is a skill, not a moral failing.
