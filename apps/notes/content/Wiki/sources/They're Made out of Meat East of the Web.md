# They're Made out of Meat | East of the Web

## Takeaways
- Terry Bisson's classic short story: alien intelligence discovers humans are **entirely meat**—born meat, die meat, brains made of meat—yet transmit radio via machines meat built.
- Dialogue format exposes absurdity of "thinking meat" and consciousness embodied in flesh; aliens reluctant to contact sentient meat directly.
- Humans use machines to talk to the stars; signals aren't from the meat itself—layered irony about substrate vs. capability.
- Cultural touchstone for sci-fi humor and philosophical alien-POV on human cognition; source material for Leiter's "They're Made Out of Weights" parody.
