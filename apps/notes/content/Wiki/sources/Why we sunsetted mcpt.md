# Why we sunsetted mcpt

## Takeaways
- Mintlify built **mcpt.com** (MCP server registry) in one week after viral MCP generator launch; shut down **five days later** despite ~3,000 day-one visitors.
- Three fatal reasons: marketplace ops exceeded lean-team capacity; diverted focus from core docs/dev-tools product; **marketplace building isn't Mintlify's strength**.
- Validation still useful—proved MCP discoverability demand—but focus beat momentum; share the "messy middle" of sunsetting moderate successes.
- Lesson: easy to lose north star during rapid growth; experiments OK if you pull plug quickly when misfit.

## Notes
Han Wang post-mortem; relevant counterweight to "build every MCP directory" instinct. Pairs with vault Raindrop-as-remote-bookmarks (no local MCP registry) stance.
